Our Story

From Vinyl to Versatility

In 2005, our founder Marcus Reed was a touring DJ who realized that great sound isn't just about the speakers—it's about the soul of the room. He began soldering his own cables and customizing lighting rigs in a garage workshop.

That "DJ-first" philosophy remains our heartbeat. We don't just supply equipment; we orchestrate atmosphere. Every lumen and every decibel is tuned with the rhythmic precision of a master set.
